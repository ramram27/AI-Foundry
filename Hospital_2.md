### Hospital / Doctor Appointment System
Concept: Online platform connecting patients, doctors, and hospital administrators.

Features:
Patient registration
Doctor registration
Doctor profiles
Specialization management
Appointment booking
Available time slots
Appointment cancellation
Patient medical records
Prescription management
Doctor dashboard
Admin dashboard
Payment integration
Email/SMS notifications

Tech Stack: React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, JWT, Redux Toolkit, Cloudinary, Razorpay
