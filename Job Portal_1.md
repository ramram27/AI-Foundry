## 3. 💼 Job Portal & Recruitment System
Create a job portal where companies can post job vacancies and applicants can search and apply for jobs. Admins manage users, companies, and job postings.

# Requirements
User registration/login
Roles: Admin, Recruiter, Job Seeker
Recruiter profile
Job seeker profile
Recruiter can create/update/delete jobs
Job search
Search by location, salary, category, and experience
Job details page
Apply for jobs
Resume upload
Application tracking
Recruiter can view applicants
Application status:
Applied
Shortlisted
Interview
Selected
Rejected
Admin dashboard
